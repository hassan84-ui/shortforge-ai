ShortForge AI — Privacy Notice

Effective: 1 October 2026

ShortForge processes information needed to provide the service, including account details, saved projects and usage information.

How information is used

Account information is used to sign you in and associate projects with your account. Project text may be sent to AI providers to generate scripts or narration. Search terms may be sent to stock-media providers to find clips. Payment and subscription processing is handled by Stripe; ShortForge does not need to store your full card details.

Storage and security

Saved account and project information is stored on the service infrastructure. Technical safeguards are used, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can choose what content to upload or save. Applicable data-protection law may give you additional rights regarding your personal information.

Third-party services

ShortForge currently relies on Stripe for payments, OpenAI for AI features, Pixabay for stock-media search and Railway for hosting. Their processing is also subject to their own privacy terms.
